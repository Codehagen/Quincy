## Trend Alerts had promised a live topic since the catalogue shipped

It runs now, and it reads where reading is free. X removed its free tier in
February, and a trend scan is only useful broad — broad on X is roughly 300
posts a day, about $45 a month against a $49 plan, which is the one rhythm
whose arithmetic simply refuses. Hacker News needs no key at all and is
earlier, which is what the promise was actually about; public GitHub search is
free too.

It leaves angles rather than an alert. A notification about somebody else's
news is a thing to feel behind on; an angle is a thing to write, and the
decision stays yours.

## Most mornings it keeps nothing, and the card says which bar they failed

Reading fifty loud topics and publishing none of them is the correct outcome
on an ordinary Tuesday. "Read 41 topics, none you have standing on" is what a
working rhythm looks like, and a run of quiet days should read that way rather
than as something broken. Nothing is judged twice: a story already turned into
an angle is never shown to the model again.
